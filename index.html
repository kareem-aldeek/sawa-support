FAQ

Common questions

Can't find an answer? Reach out directly and we'll get back to you as soon as possible.

How do I connect with my partner? +

After creating your account, go to the Partner section and share your unique invite link or code. Once they accept, you'll both be connected inside the app.

Is my data private and secure? +

Yes. Sawa is built exclusively for you and your partner. Your messages, journal entries, and shared content are only visible to the two of you and are never shared with third parties.

I can't sign in to my account. What should I do? +

Try these steps: (1) Make sure you're using the correct phone number or email. (2) Request a new OTP if the code expired. (3) Check your internet connection. If the issue persists, contact us and we'll help you recover your account.

How do I delete my account? +

Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account inside the app. This permanently removes all your data including messages and journal entries. This action cannot be undone.

Notifications are not working — how do I fix this? +

Go to your iPhone's Settings → Sawa → Notifications and make sure Allow Notifications is on. Also check that Do Not Disturb is not active. If the issue continues, try logging out and back in to refresh your push token.

Can I use Sawa on multiple devices? +

Currently Sawa is optimized for one device per account. Multi-device support is on our roadmap and will be available in a future update.

How do I report a bug or send feedback? +

Send an email to support@sawaapp.com with a description of the issue, your device model, and iOS version. Screenshots are very helpful!

What iOS version does Sawa require? +

Sawa requires iOS 16 or later. For the best experience, keep your device updated to the latest iOS version.
