Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I invite my partner? — Open the app, go to Settings, and tap "Invite Partner" to share your unique code.
  • Is my data private? — Yes. All your shared data is private and only visible to you and your partner.
  • How do I delete my account? — Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account.
