Private by design

Only the two of you

Sawa connects exactly two people. There is no feed, no followers and no one else to share with.

Yours alone

Only the two of you can see anything you share in the app.

Paired by a code

One of you shares a code, the other enters it. That is the whole of it.

No location history

If you share your location, only your latest position is kept, never a history. Turning sharing off deletes it.

FAQ

Common questions

Can't find your answer? Write to us and we'll help.

How do I connect with my partner?

Tap Set Up Connection on Home. One of you shares an invite code and the other enters it. Once the code is accepted, you are connected.

Who can see what I share?

Only you and your partner. Everything you share in Sawa is visible to the two of you and nobody else. You can read the details in our Privacy Policy.

I can't sign in or I didn't get my code

Sawa signs you in with your phone number and a 6-digit code sent by text message. Right now it accepts Jordanian mobile numbers: 9 digits starting with 7. Check the number, check your connection, then choose Send a new code when the timer ends. If it still doesn't work, email us and we'll look into it.

How do I unpair?

Open Profile and choose Unpair.

How do I delete my account and data?

Email info@sawacouples.com and ask for your account to be deleted, including the phone number you signed in with. Deleting an account removes the data stored for it, and this can't be undone.

Notifications aren't arriving

On your iPhone, open Settings → Sawa → Notifications and make sure notifications are allowed, and that a Focus mode isn't silencing them. Inside the app, Profile → Notifications lets you choose what you get.

Can I use Sawa in Arabic and English?

Yes. Choose Profile → Language, then restart the app to finish switching. Profile → Appearance sets light, dark, or matching your device.

How do I report a problem or send feedback?

Email info@sawacouples.com with what happened, your iPhone model and iOS version. Screenshots help a lot.

Which iPhones does Sawa support?

Sawa is an iPhone app and needs iOS 15.1 or later. Keeping iOS up to date gives you the best experience.
